Salama! — part of the Vahiny Mianatra Malagasy project. Lessons and curriculum are original. Session audio is the public-domain Peace Corps Malagasy Language Lessons; single words and dialogue lines are synthesised with Meta's MMS Malagasy voice (facebook/mms-tts-mlg, CC BY-NC 4.0). Some traveler phrases and the pronunciation guide are adapted from Wikivoyage's Malagasy phrasebook (CC BY-SA 3.0).
