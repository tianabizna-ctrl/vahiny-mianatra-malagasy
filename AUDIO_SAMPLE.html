Salama! — rendered Malagasy audio

16 clips spoken by Meta's MMS Malagasy voice model (facebook/mms-tts-mlg, CC BY-NC 4.0), not by a fallback voice with a foreign accent. These are the clips the app now plays before it reaches for any device voice. Judge the stress and the vowels, not the recording quality: the model is 16 kHz mono and 32 kbps.
