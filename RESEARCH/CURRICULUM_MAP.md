# Curriculum Map — 12-Week Course for an Elderly American Beginner

**Cadence:** 3 sessions/week (Mon/Wed/Fri), 90 minutes each (from your FIANARANA/MIANATRA docs).
**Every session:** 20–25 min dialogue practice → 35 min phonetics/vocabulary → 30 min roleplay/games.
**Elderly-friendly principles baked in:** large print, short lists, spaced repetition (review at end of
session → after 3 days → after 1 week), TPR, image-first flashcards (never English text on cards),
praise over correction, no passive voice in the first 3 months.

Legend:
- 🎧 = Peace Corps audio (in `MALAGASY ASSETS/Malagasy-Sakalava/`)
- 📘 = Bako lesson text (in `extracted/bako-text/` — Introductory `int`, Grammar `gram`, Theme `theme`)
- 📄 = Reference PDF


## Month 1 — The Absolute Basics

### Week 1 — Pronunciation & Greetings
| Session | Content | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Greetings dialogue: *Salama! / Manao ahoana? / Tsara fa misaotra* | 🎧 Intro + L1‑2 · 📘 int 1a |
| Wed | Vowels a, e, i, o; letters J, O; *Misaotra / Azafady* | 🎧 L1 (alphabet) · 📘 int 1b |
| Fri | Review: full greeting + goodbye dialogue; **12-week goal-setting** | 🎧 L2 · 📘 int 2 |

### Week 2 — Pronouns & Introducing Yourself
| Session | Content | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | *Izaho, ianao, izy*; "Miteny malagasy ve ianao?" — "Kely fotsiny" | 📘 int 3 |
| Wed | *Izahay, isika* (exclusive vs inclusive "we") — fun for an English speaker! | 📘 gram 12, 13a |
| Fri | Dialogue: name, origin, job, family basics (*Manambady aho. Manana zanaka aho.*) | 🎧 L3 |

### Week 3 — Numbers & Money
| Session | Content | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Numbers 1–10, *Firy?* | 🎧 L9 · 📘 theme 10 |
| Wed | 11–100, right-to-left reading, ariary | 🎧 L9 · 📘 int 4 |
| Fri | Market dialogue: *Ohatrinona ity? Lafo be! Mora ve?* | 📘 int 4 + int review |

### Week 4 — VOS Sentence Structure
| Session | Content | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | *Mihinana vary aho* — VOS order; verbs *mihinana, misotro, mandeha* | 🎧 L5 · 📘 gram 1 |
| Wed | Negation with *tsy* | 📘 gram 1 |
| Fri | **Month 1 review** — market roleplay game | 📘 int review exercise + gram 1 |


## Month 2 — Everyday Life

### Week 5 — Food & Ordering
| Mon | Food/drink vocabulary | 🎧 L10 · 📘 theme 8a |
| Wed | *Mila … / Te hi…* constructions | 📘 int 2, gram 5 |
| Fri | Restaurant dialogue + ordering | dialogue practice |

### Week 6 — Directions & Places
| Session | Content | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | *Aiza ny …?* (toilet, market, bank) | 🎧 L6 · 📘 theme 11a |
| Wed | *Ankavanana, ankavia, mahitsy* (right/left/straight); *ato, ao, any* (near/mid/far) | 📘 gram 14a |
| Fri | Map roleplay: get from home to market | 📘 theme 11b·d |

### Week 7 — Family & Possessives
| Session | Content | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Family members; family photos session (she brings hers!) | 🎧 L4 · 📘 theme 1 |
| Wed | Possessive suffixes -ko, -nao, -ny: *tranoko, vadinao, zanany* | 📘 gram 13a·b·d |
| Fri | Her family tree in Malagasy — show & tell | 📘 theme 1 |

### Week 8 — Time & Calendar
| Session | Content | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Days of the week; *omaly, androany, rahampitso* | 🎧 L11 · 📘 gram 15a |
| Wed | Clock: *Amin'ny firy izao?* (also the Malagasy habit of counting evening hours from the evening, 6 p.m. = 12) | 📘 theme 10 |
| Fri | Making appointments: *Hihaona amin'ny Zoma amin'ny efatra isika* | 📘 gram 15b·d |


## Month 3 — Immersion & Real Use

### Week 9 — Tenses (Past & Future)
| Session | Content | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | M- → N- (present → past): *mihinana → nihinana* | 📘 gram 1, 2 |
| Wed | M- → H- (present → future): *mihinana → hihinana* | 📘 gram 2 |
| Fri | *Inona no nataonao omaly?* / *Inona no hataonao rahampitso?* | dialogue practice |

### Week 10 — Health & Emergencies
| Session | Content | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Body parts (*lohako, kiboko, tongotro*) | 📘 theme 7a |
| Wed | *Marary aho! Vonjeo! Mila dokotera aho!* | 🎧 L6 · 📘 theme 7b |
| Fri | Emergency roleplay (important for an elderly student!) | dialogue practice |

### Week 11 — Small Talk & Weather
| Session | Content | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Weather: *mafana, mangatsiaka, avy ny orana* | 📘 theme 3 |
| Wed | Reactions & opinions: *Tsara kely! Mahafinaritra! Sarotra! Mora!* | 🎧 L12 |
| Fri | Free conversation about hobbies/likes | 📘 theme 6 (clothing/colours for descriptions) |

### Week 12 — Grand Finale
| Session | Content | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Market simulation — buy a week of groceries entirely in Malagasy | everything! |
| Wed | Transport roleplay: taxi-brousse | 📘 theme 15a·b |
| Fri | **45 minutes conversation ONLY in Malagasy** + certificate & celebration | 🎉 |


## Beyond 12 weeks — continuing content
The Bako thematic lessons not used above can fuel months 4–6: the house (theme 2), kitchen (4),
jobs (5), school (12), chores (13), daily routine (14), animals (9), plus deeper grammar tracks
(gram 3–11: transitive/intransitive verbs, adverbs, conjunctions, comparisons, demonstratives).
